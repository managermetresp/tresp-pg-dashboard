Segregation of the combined dataset by District / DPMU Zone, Block and Category (Fishery / Livestock / Agri-Horti — the PG's commodity type, which is which of the 69 source sheets a member came from). "District" here is the source sheet's DPMU project zone (West/Dhalai/Khowai/Gomati/North), not always the block's literal revenue district — e.g. Jampuijala block sits in Sepahijala district but reports under DPMU West. This is also distinct from "Activity" elsewhere in this dashboard, which refers to the loan product (Nursery/Polyculture) that drives the repayment schedule.

A member is marked defaulting if either: (a) they made zero repayments even though their moratorium period is over — Livestock repays from the 2nd month (1-month moratorium), Fishery from the 4th (3 months), Agri-Horti from the 5th (4 months) — or (b) the cumulative amount they've paid so far is less than what should have been paid by now (based on the official TRESP repayment schedule for their loan product — Agri, Polyculture, Nursery, PPU, GPU, GRU or PFU — rebuilt from their own disbursement amounts and dates). This is amount-based, not count-based: a member who skips a month but later pays double to catch up is not flagged, since what matters is whether they're caught up on the total, not whether every single month was paid on time. A member is marked Completed instead of defaulting once their total paid reaches or exceeds the amount they received.

What this page tracks, day by day: New names added: a member's name appearing in a sheet for the first time. The sheets don't record when a row was typed in, so the dashboard keeps its own log (loading…). Names already in the sheets when tracking started are the starting list and are not counted as new. Each sync adds today's new names, so open or sync the dashboard at least once a day. Repayments: every monthly repayment amount in the sheets, placed on the repayment date entered beside it. "Instalment" is the month column it was entered under (Month 1, Month 2…), and "Due in" is the calendar month that instalment falls due, counted from the member's first disbursement date. Loans disbursed: members whose first disbursement date falls on that day. The filters above apply. Click a day in the table to see only that day.

Repayment entered but no loan amount received
These rows have a repayment entered but the amount received (column S) is ₹0 — often sanctioned is blank too. The repayment still counts in every "repaid" figure on the dashboard (Overview, District, Block, Category, Sub-category and PG-wise), since the money was paid. They are not counted as loan members and have no due amount until the amount received is filled in at the source.

Wrong sub-activity: tenure code doesn't match the PG type
The "Tenure (in Months)" code (column Y) belongs to a different activity than the sheet or the PG's "Type of PG" (column G). For example, an Agri/Horti code on a Piggery PG, a pig code (PPU/PFU) on a Goatery PG, or a livestock code in the Fishery sheet. The dashboard picks each member's repayment schedule from this code, so members with a loan are on the wrong schedule until the code is corrected. Expected codes: Agri-Horti → Agri/Horti; Fishery → Polyculture or Nursery; Piggery → PPU or PFU; Goatery → GPU or GRU. Members without a loan yet are listed too, so the code can be fixed before money is released.
