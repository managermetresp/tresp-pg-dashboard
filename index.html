This is also distinct from "Activity" elsewhere in this dashboard, which refers to the loan product (Nursery/Polyculture) that drives the repayment schedule.

A member is marked defaulting if either: (a) they made zero repayments even though their moratorium period is over — Livestock repays from the 2nd month (1-month moratorium), Fishery from the 4th (3 months), Agri-Horti from the 5th (4 months) — or (b) the cumulative amount they've paid so far is less than what should have been paid by now (based on an even monthly EMI over their activity's repayment window). This is amount-based, not count-based: a member who skips a month but later pays double to catch up is not flagged, since what matters is whether they're caught up on the total, not whether every single month was paid on time. A member is marked Completed instead of defaulting once their total paid reaches or exceeds the amount they received.

Members whose Loan Amount Sanctioned is more than the amount actually disbursed (Total amount Received in Kind and Cash). Pending = Sanctioned − Disbursed (disbursed = "Total amount Received in Kind and Cash", column S); it is money still to be released and is not part of what the member has to repay. (The sheet's column U is Sanctioned − Utilized, a different figure, and is not used here.) Not disbursed = nothing released yet (these members are not counted on the other pages); Partly disbursed = some money released, the rest still pending (these are counted on the other pages for the amount released).
